About — Ye Site Kyun Banai Gayi?

Pakistan me log Islamic date, namaz timings aur Ramadan timetable ke liye rozana search karte hain — lekin aksar websites par ya to sirf baray shehron ki timings hoti hain, ya Islamic date ghalat waqt (aadhi raat) par badalti hai, ya content English me hota hai. Pakistan Islam Portal in gaps ko poora karne ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Is Site Par Kya Milta Hai?

  • 160+ shehron ki namaz timings — districts ke saath choti cities aur tehsils bhi.
  • Hanafi + Jafria timings har sheher ke page par.
  • Maghrib rule — Islamic date Maghrib ke baad badalti hai, jese Islami usool hai.
  • Ramadan 2027 ka overview aur har sheher ka 30 din ka timetable.
  • Surah tarjuma Roman Urdu me, Islamic events 2027 aur articles mukammal Urdu script me.

Timings Ka Method Kya Hai?

Timings University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi ke method (Fajr aur Isha ke liye 18 degree) aur Hanafi Asr par mabni hain. Jafria timings Shia Ithna-Ashari method se li jati hain. Data Aladhan API (free) se aata hai aur rozana khud bakhud update hota hai. Mahine ka aaghaz Ruet-e-Hilal Committee ke elaan se confirm hota hai — calculated date aur official date me 1 din ka farq ho sakta hai.

Zaban Kyun Roman Urdu + Urdu Script?

Timings aur Surah tarjuma Roman Urdu me hain kyunke log isi zaban me search karte hain. Tafseeli articles mukammal Urdu script (Nastaliq) me hain taake parhne me aasani aur adab dono rahen.
