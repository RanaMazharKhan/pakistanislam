🌙 Lahore Ka Ramadan 2027 Timetable

Lahore Ki Timings Ke Baare Me

Lahore ki namaz timings University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi ke method (Fajr aur Isha ke liye 18 degree) par mabni hain aur Asr Hanafi tareeqe se nikali jati hai — yehi method Pakistan me aam tor par istemal hota hai. Sehri ka aakhri waqt Fajr hai aur iftar Maghrib ke waqt hota hai. Islamic date Maghrib ke baad badal jati hai, is liye is page par Maghrib ke baad agle din ki Hijri date dikhai jati hai. Timings rozana khud bakhud update hoti hain, phir bhi apni local masjid ki timing ko tarjeeh den.

Lahore — Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Lahore me aaj sehri kab khatam hogi?

Sehri Fajr ke time par khatam hoti hai — aaj Lahore me Fajr 04:54 par hai. Sehri us se pehle mukammal kar len.

Lahore me aaj iftar kab hoga?

Iftar Maghrib ke waqt hota hai — aaj Lahore me Maghrib 17:17 par hai. Jafria iftar is se chand minute baad hota hai (upar Jafria table dekhen).

Lahore ki timings ka method kya hai?

Karachi method (18°/18°) aur Hanafi Asr. Yehi method Pakistan ke aksar shehron me istemal hota hai.
