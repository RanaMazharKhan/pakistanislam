Har Event Ki Tafseel

Shab-e-Miraj woh raat hai jis me Nabi ﷺ ko Masjid-e-Haram se Masjid-e-Aqsa aur phir aasmanon ki sair karayi gayi. Shab-e-Barat Shaban ki 15wi shab hai, jis me log nafil ibadat, dua aur astaghfar karte hain. Ashura Muharram ki 10 tareekh hai — is din roza rakhna masnoon hai. Eid Milad-un-Nabi Nabi ﷺ ki wiladat ka din hai, jisay log durood, zikr aur khushi se manate hain.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Islamic dates har saal kyun badal jati hain?

Kyunki Islamic calendar chand par chalta hai aur qamri saal angrezi saal se taqreeban 11 din chhota hota hai — is liye har event har saal 10–11 din pehle aa jata hai.

Final date kab confirm hoti hai?

Chand nazar aane ke baad Ruet-e-Hilal Committee elaan karti hai. Us se pehle tamam dates sirf andaze hoti hain.

Pakistan aur Saudi Arab ki dates me farq kyun hota hai?

Chand nazar aane ka waqt aur jagah farq hoti hai — is liye ek mulk me mahina 1 din pehle ya baad me shuru ho sakta hai.
