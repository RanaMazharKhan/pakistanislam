🕐 Sehri aur Iftar Ka Tareeqa

Sehri Fajr ki azan se pehle khatam hoti hai — yani sehri ka aakhri waqt Fajr ka time hai. Iftar Maghrib ke waqt hota hai, jese hi sooraj ghuroob ho. Jafria fiqh me iftar Maghrib ke baad surkhi khatam hone par hota hai, is liye chand minute late hota hai — har sheher ke page par dono timings maujood hain.

🌟 Ramadan Ki Tayyari

Ramadan se pehle apne sheher ka timetable save kar len, masjid ki timings se match kar len, aur chand raat ko Ruet-e-Hilal ka elaan zaroor sunen — kyunke elaan ke baad hi pehle Roze ki final date tay hoti hai. Sehri me khajoor, pani aur halki ghiza behtar rehti hai, aur iftar khajoor aur pani se kholna sunnat hai.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Ramadan 2027 kab shuru hoga?

8 February 2027 mutawaqqa hai, Pakistan me 9 February bhi mumkin hai. Final elaan Ruet-e-Hilal se hoga.

Sehri kab tak kha sakte hain?

Fajr ke time tak. Fajr ka time guzar jaye to sehri ka waqt khatam — is liye 5–10 minute pehle ruk jana behtar hai.

Jafria iftar late kyun hota hai?

Jafria fiqh me iftar Maghrib ke baad surkhi khatam hone par hota hai, is liye chand minute ka farq hota hai.

Poora timetable kahan milega?

Upar wale button ya Ramadan index se apna sheher chunen — poora 30 din ka timetable milega.
