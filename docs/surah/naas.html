Surah Naas (114) — Mukammal Tarjuma

6 aayat • Madani Surah • Quran ki aakhri Surah

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ۝ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ۝ إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِن شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ ۝ الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ

Roman Urdu Tarjuma

  1. Kaho: Main panah mangta hoon logon ke Rab ki.
  2. Logon ke Badshah ki.
  3. Logon ke Mabood ki.
  4. Waswasa dalne wale ke shar se jo (Allah ka naam sun kar) peeche hat jata hai.
  5. Jo logon ke dilon me waswasa dalta hai.
  6. Khwah woh jinno me se ho ya insano me se.

Fazilat

Surah Naas waswason aur bure khayalat se hifazat ke liye parhi jati hai. Subah shaam teen teen dafa Surah Ikhlas, Falaq aur Naas parhna masnoon hai aur ye har shar se kifayat karti hain.

Is Surah Ko Kab Parhen?

Subah shaam, sone se pehle, aur jab dil me waswase aayen. Wazu ke baad aur namaz ke baad bhi parhna behtar hai.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Waswasa aaye to kya karen?

Allah ki panah mangen, Surah Naas parhen aur us khayal ko tawajjo na den — waswasa aam tor par shaitan ki taraf se hota hai aur is par amal na karna hi is ka ilaaj hai.

Surah Naas Quran ki aakhri Surah kyun hai?

Quran ki tartib me ye aakhri Surah hai — is me insan ko har shar se Allah ki panah mangne ki taleem di gayi hai, jo Quran ke paigham ka khulasa hai.
