1. Timings Calculated Hain

Is site ki namaz timings University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi ke method se calculate ki jati hain. Maqami farq (masjid ki apni setting, sheher ke andar ka farq) ki wajah se chand minute ka farq ho sakta hai. Apni local masjid ki timing ko hi final samjhen.

2. Islamic Dates Aur Ruet-e-Hilal

Ramadan, Eid aur baqi events ki dates mutawaqqa (expected) hain. Final date sirf Markazi Ruet-e-Hilal Committee ke elaan ya hukoomati notification se confirm hoti hai. Koi bhi faisla (roza, Eid) sirf official elaan par karen.

3. Deeni Maloomat Fatwa Nahi

Articles aur Surah tarjuma samajhne aur rehnumai ke liye hain — ye fatwa nahi hain. Kisi masle me shari hidayat ke liye apne ilaqe ke mustanad aalim se ruju karen. Tarjume me koi ghalti nazar aaye to rabta karen, islah kar di jayegi.

4. Zimmedari Ki Hudood

Hum maloomat ko durust rakhne ki poori koshish karte hain, lekin kisi ghalti ya waqti kharabi (misal: API down hona) se hone wale nuqsan ke zimmedar nahi honge. Bahar ke links ki content ke liye hum jawabdeh nahi.
