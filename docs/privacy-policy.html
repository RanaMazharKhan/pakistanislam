🔒 Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: 28 October 2026

1. Hum Kya Data Collect Karte Hain?

Is site par koi account banane ki zaroorat nahi, aur hum aap ka naam, phone number, address ya koi personal maloomat khud se collect nahi karte. Aap sirf tab apni maloomat dete hain jab aap khud Contact form bharte hain (naam aur email) — wo maloomat sirf aap ke paigham ka jawab dene ke liye istemal hoti hai, kisi teesre ko nahi di jati.

2. Cookies Aur Local Storage

Star-rating widget aap ki rating sirf aap ke apne browser me save karta hai (localStorage) — ye rating hamare server tak nahi jati aur kisi aur ko nazar nahi aati. Mustaqbil me is site par ishtehaar (Google AdSense) aa sakte hain, jo apni cookies ke zariye dilchaspiyon ke mutabiq ishtehaar dikha sakte hain. Aap apne browser ki settings se cookies ko kabhi bhi delete ya block kar sakte hain.

3. Third-Party Services

Namaz timings Aladhan API se li jati hain. Jab aap demo pages kholte hain to aap ka browser seedha Aladhan se data leta hai — is doran Aladhan ki apni privacy policy laagu hoti hai. Is site par maujood bahar ke links (misal: GitHub, Formspree) ki apni policies hain.

4. Bachon Ki Privacy

Ye site bachon se jaan boojh kar koi personal maloomat collect nahi karti. Agar walidain ko lage ke un ke bache ne contact form se maloomat bhej di hai to rabta karen, hum usay delete kar denge.

5. Policy Me Tabdeeli

Ye policy waqt ke saath update ho sakti hai. Bari tabdeeli ki soorat me is page par nayi tareekh likh di jayegi.

6. Rabta

Privacy se mutalliq koi sawal ho to Contact page se rabta karen.
