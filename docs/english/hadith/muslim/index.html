📜 Sahih Muslim

Sahih Muslim is mentioned together with Sahih al-Bukhari in rank of authenticity; the two together are called “the two Sahihs” (al-Sahihan). Imam Muslim took care to gather the different chains of transmission of a single hadith in one place, which makes it easy to compare the wordings of the text. His famous introduction at the beginning of the book is an important source for the principles of hadith science.

Compiler: Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj (204–261 AH)
Total hadiths: 7563 — Books (chapters): 57
