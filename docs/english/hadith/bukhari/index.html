📜 Sahih al-Bukhari

Sahih al-Bukhari is regarded by the scholars of the ummah as the most authentic book after the Holy Quran. Imam al-Bukhari applied extremely strict conditions in compiling it — every narrator had to be trustworthy, and his direct hearing (sama') from his teacher had to be established. The book arranges the hadiths under juristic chapter headings, and those headings themselves (tarajim al-abwab) are a mirror of Imam al-Bukhari's deep juristic insight.

Compiler: Imam Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari (194–256 AH)
Total hadiths: 7589 — Books (chapters): 97
