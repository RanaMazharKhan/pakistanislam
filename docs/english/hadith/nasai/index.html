📜 Sunan an-Nasa'i

Badshahi Mosque, Lahore — Photo: Wikimedia Commons (Creative Commons)

Sunan an-Nasa'i is famous for strictness in examining chains of transmission. Because of the standard Imam an-Nasa'i maintained in criticising and accrediting narrators (al-jarh wa-t-ta'dil), his Sunan holds a special place among the books of Sunan.

Compiler: Imam Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb an-Nasa'i (215–303 AH)
Total hadiths: 5765 — Books (chapters): 51
