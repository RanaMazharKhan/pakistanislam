📜 Sunan Abu Dawud

Sunan Abu Dawud is one of the most important collections of hadiths concerning juristic and practical rulings. Imam Abu Dawud mostly collected those hadiths from which the jurists derive legal rulings, and where he considered a hadith to contain some weakness, he often pointed that out as well.

Compiler: Imam Abu Dawud as-Sijistani (202–275 AH)
Total hadiths: 5274 — Books (chapters): 43
