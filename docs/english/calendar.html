Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Islamic date today?

Today's Islamic (Hijri) date is shown in large letters at the very top of this page — this is also today's lunar (Chand) date. It follows the calculated calendar; in Pakistan the final announcement of a new month is made by the Central Ruet-e-Hilal Committee, so a difference of one day is possible.

What is today's Arabic date?

By "Arabic date" the Hijri (lunar) date itself is meant — the Hijri date given above is today's Arabic date.

How many days are there in a Hijri year?

A Hijri year consists of 12 lunar months and has 354 or 355 days — that is why Ramadan and the Eids come about 10 to 11 days earlier in the Gregorian calendar every year.

Is the converter's date final?

No. It is a calculated date; in Pakistan the start of a month depends on sighting the moon, and the final announcement is made by the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee, so a difference of one day is possible.

What are the Islamic months?

Muharram, Safar, Rabi al-Awwal, Rabi al-Thani, Jumada al-Ula, Jumada al-Akhirah, Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal, Dhul-Qa'dah and Dhul-Hijjah.
