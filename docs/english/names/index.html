👶 Islamic Names — Allah's Names and Good Names for Children

A good name is a child's first gift and a parent's responsibility. The Hadith encourages giving good names, and among the most beloved names are those expressing servitude to Allah (Abdullah, Abdur Rahman) and the names of the Prophets. On this page, Allah's 99 names and selected Islamic names for children are given with their meanings.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind When Choosing a Name

Look for a good meaning in the name. Naming after the Prophets, the Companions and the Ahl al-Bayt is liked, and avoid names whose meanings are bad or in which shirk or arrogance shows. For a final opinion on a name, also consult your local scholar.
