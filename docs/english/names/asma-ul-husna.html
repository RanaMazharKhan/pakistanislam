🌙 Asma-ul-Husna — The 99 Names of Allah with Meanings

These 99 names of Allah Almighty are arranged according to the well-known narration that has come in Jami' at-Tirmidhi: "Allah has ninety-nine names; whoever learns them (and understands what they require) will enter Paradise." How Should Asma-ul-Husna Be Read?

Using these names as a means in dua is in accordance with the guidance of the Quran: "Allah has the best names, so call upon Him by them" (Surah Al-A'raf: 180). Choose the name that fits the occasion — for provision, make dua keeping the meaning of Ar-Razzaq in your heart; for healing, Ash-Shafi — and try to adopt what every name requires in your own life.
