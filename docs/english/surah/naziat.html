Surah An-Naazi'aat — Those who drag forth

Surah number79 of 114
Total ayahs46
RevelationMakki (Makkah)
Juz (Para)30

Full Arabic text (Uthmani script) with the Saheeh International English translation, ayah by ayah.

Surah An-Naazi'aat — Arabic & English Translation

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Ayah 1

وَٱلنَّٰزِعَٰتِ غَرْقًۭا

By those [angels] who extract with violence

Ayah 2

وَٱلنَّٰشِطَٰتِ نَشْطًۭا

And [by] those who remove with ease

Ayah 3

وَٱلسَّٰبِحَٰتِ سَبْحًۭا

And [by] those who glide [as if] swimming

Ayah 4

فَٱلسَّٰبِقَٰتِ سَبْقًۭا

And those who race each other in a race

Ayah 5

فَٱلْمُدَبِّرَٰتِ أَمْرًۭا

And those who arrange [each] matter,

Ayah 6

يَوْمَ تَرْجُفُ ٱلرَّاجِفَةُ

On the Day the blast [of the Horn] will convulse [creation],

Ayah 7

تَتْبَعُهَا ٱلرَّادِفَةُ

There will follow it the subsequent [one].

Ayah 8

قُلُوبٌۭ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ وَاجِفَةٌ

Hearts, that Day, will tremble,

Ayah 9

أَبْصَٰرُهَا خَٰشِعَةٌۭ

Their eyes humbled.

Ayah 10

يَقُولُونَ أَءِنَّا لَمَرْدُودُونَ فِى ٱلْحَافِرَةِ

They are [presently] saying, "Will we indeed be returned to [our] former state [of life]?

Ayah 11

أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمًۭا نَّخِرَةًۭ

Even if we should be decayed bones?

Ayah 12

قَالُوا۟ تِلْكَ إِذًۭا كَرَّةٌ خَاسِرَةٌۭ

They say, "That, then, would be a losing return."

Ayah 13

فَإِنَّمَا هِىَ زَجْرَةٌۭ وَٰحِدَةٌۭ

Indeed, it will be but one shout,

Ayah 14

فَإِذَا هُم بِٱلسَّاهِرَةِ

And suddenly they will be [alert] upon the earth's surface.

Ayah 15

هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ

Has there reached you the story of Moses? -

Ayah 16

إِذْ نَادَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ بِٱلْوَادِ ٱلْمُقَدَّسِ طُوًى

When his Lord called to him in the sacred valley of Tuwa,

Ayah 17

ٱذْهَبْ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ

"Go to Pharaoh. Indeed, he has transgressed.

Ayah 18

فَقُلْ هَل لَّكَ إِلَىٰٓ أَن تَزَكَّىٰ

And say to him, 'Would you [be willing to] purify yourself

Ayah 19

وَأَهْدِيَكَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَتَخْشَىٰ

And let me guide you to your Lord so you would fear [Him]?'"

Ayah 20

فَأَرَىٰهُ ٱلْءَايَةَ ٱلْكُبْرَىٰ

And he showed him the greatest sign,

Ayah 21

فَكَذَّبَ وَعَصَىٰ

But Pharaoh denied and disobeyed.

Ayah 22

ثُمَّ أَدْبَرَ يَسْعَىٰ

Then he turned his back, striving.

Ayah 23

فَحَشَرَ فَنَادَىٰ

And he gathered [his people] and called out

Ayah 24

فَقَالَ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلْأَعْلَىٰ

And said, "I am your most exalted lord."

Ayah 25

فَأَخَذَهُ ٱللَّهُ نَكَالَ ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ وَٱلْأُولَىٰٓ

So Allah seized him in exemplary punishment for the last and the first [transgression].

Ayah 26

إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبْرَةًۭ لِّمَن يَخْشَىٰٓ

Indeed in that is a warning for whoever would fear [Allah].

Ayah 27

ءَأَنتُمْ أَشَدُّ خَلْقًا أَمِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ ۚ بَنَىٰهَا

Are you a more difficult creation or is the heaven? Allah constructed it.

Ayah 28

رَفَعَ سَمْكَهَا فَسَوَّىٰهَا

He raised its ceiling and proportioned it.

Ayah 29

وَأَغْطَشَ لَيْلَهَا وَأَخْرَجَ ضُحَىٰهَا

And He darkened its night and extracted its brightness.

Ayah 30

وَٱلْأَرْضَ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ دَحَىٰهَآ

And after that He spread the earth.

Ayah 31

أَخْرَجَ مِنْهَا مَآءَهَا وَمَرْعَىٰهَا

He extracted from it its water and its pasture,

Ayah 32

وَٱلْجِبَالَ أَرْسَىٰهَا

And the mountains He set firmly

Ayah 33

مَتَٰعًۭا لَّكُمْ وَلِأَنْعَٰمِكُمْ

As provision for you and your grazing livestock.

Ayah 34

فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلطَّآمَّةُ ٱلْكُبْرَىٰ

But when there comes the greatest Overwhelming Calamity -

Ayah 35

يَوْمَ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مَا سَعَىٰ

The Day when man will remember that for which he strove,

Ayah 36

وَبُرِّزَتِ ٱلْجَحِيمُ لِمَن يَرَىٰ

And Hellfire will be exposed for [all] those who see -

Ayah 37

فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ

So as for he who transgressed

Ayah 38

وَءَاثَرَ ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا

And preferred the life of the world,

Ayah 39

فَإِنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ هِىَ ٱلْمَأْوَىٰ

Then indeed, Hellfire will be [his] refuge.

Ayah 40

وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفْسَ عَنِ ٱلْهَوَىٰ

But as for he who feared the position of his Lord and prevented the soul from [unlawful] inclination,

Ayah 41

فَإِنَّ ٱلْجَنَّةَ هِىَ ٱلْمَأْوَىٰ

Then indeed, Paradise will be [his] refuge.

Ayah 42

يَسْـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ أَيَّانَ مُرْسَىٰهَا

They ask you, [O Muhammad], about the Hour: when is its arrival?

Ayah 43

فِيمَ أَنتَ مِن ذِكْرَىٰهَآ

In what [position] are you that you should mention it?

Ayah 44

إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ مُنتَهَىٰهَآ

To your Lord is its finality.

Ayah 45

إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرُ مَن يَخْشَىٰهَا

You are only a warner for those who fear it.

Ayah 46

كَأَنَّهُمْ يَوْمَ يَرَوْنَهَا لَمْ يَلْبَثُوٓا۟ إِلَّا عَشِيَّةً أَوْ ضُحَىٰهَا

It will be, on the Day they see it, as though they had not remained [in the world] except for an afternoon or a morning thereof.

Surah An-Naazi'aat — Frequently Asked Questions

How many ayahs are in Surah An-Naazi'aat?

Surah An-Naazi'aat has 46 ayahs. It is surah number 79 of the Holy Quran.

Where was Surah An-Naazi'aat revealed?

Surah An-Naazi'aat is a Makki surah — it was revealed in Makkah before the Hijrah to Madinah..

What does the name An-Naazi'aat mean?

The name An-Naazi'aat means “Those who drag forth” in English.
