Surah Al-Humaza — Arabic & English Translation

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Ayah 1

وَيْلٌۭ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍۢ لُّمَزَةٍ

Woe to every scorner and mocker

Ayah 2

ٱلَّذِى جَمَعَ مَالًۭا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ

Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it.

Ayah 3

يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخْلَدَهُۥ

He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

Ayah 4

كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ

No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher.

Ayah 5

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ

And what can make you know what is the Crusher?

Ayah 6

نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ

It is the fire of Allah, [eternally] fueled,

Ayah 7

ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ

Which mounts directed at the hearts.

Ayah 8

إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌۭ

Indeed, Hellfire will be closed down upon them

Ayah 9

فِى عَمَدٍۢ مُّمَدَّدَةٍۭ

In extended columns.

Surah Al-Humaza — Frequently Asked Questions

How many ayahs are in Surah Al-Humaza?

Surah Al-Humaza has 9 ayahs. It is surah number 104 of the Holy Quran.

Where was Surah Al-Humaza revealed?

Surah Al-Humaza is a Makki surah — it was revealed in Makkah before the Hijrah to Madinah..

What does the name Al-Humaza mean?

The name Al-Humaza means “The Traducer” in English.
