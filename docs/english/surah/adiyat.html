Surah Al-Aadiyaat — Arabic & English Translation

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Ayah 1

وَٱلْعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبْحًۭا

By the racers, panting,

Ayah 2

فَٱلْمُورِيَٰتِ قَدْحًۭا

And the producers of sparks [when] striking

Ayah 3

فَٱلْمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبْحًۭا

And the chargers at dawn,

Ayah 4

فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِۦ نَقْعًۭا

Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust,

Ayah 5

فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِۦ جَمْعًا

Arriving thereby in the center collectively,

Ayah 6

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٌۭ

Indeed mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful.

Ayah 7

وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌۭ

And indeed, he is to that a witness.

Ayah 8

وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ

And indeed he is, in love of wealth, intense.

Ayah 9

۞ أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ إِذَا بُعْثِرَ مَا فِى ٱلْقُبُورِ

But does he not know that when the contents of the graves are scattered

Ayah 10

وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِى ٱلصُّدُورِ

And that within the breasts is obtained,

Ayah 11

إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ لَّخَبِيرٌۢ

Indeed, their Lord with them, that Day, is [fully] Acquainted.

Surah Al-Aadiyaat — Frequently Asked Questions

How many ayahs are in Surah Al-Aadiyaat?

Surah Al-Aadiyaat has 11 ayahs. It is surah number 100 of the Holy Quran.

Where was Surah Al-Aadiyaat revealed?

Surah Al-Aadiyaat is a Makki surah — it was revealed in Makkah before the Hijrah to Madinah..

What does the name Al-Aadiyaat mean?

The name Al-Aadiyaat means “The Chargers” in English.
