Surah Al-Haaqqa — The Reality

Total ayahs52
RevelationMakki (Makkah)

Full Arabic text (Uthmani script) with the Saheeh International English translation, ayah by ayah.

Surah Al-Haaqqa — Arabic & English Translation

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Ayah 1

ٱلْحَآقَّةُ

The Inevitable Reality -

Ayah 2

مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ

What is the Inevitable Reality?

Ayah 3

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ

And what can make you know what is the Inevitable Reality?

Ayah 4

كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ

Thamud and 'Aad denied the Striking Calamity.

Ayah 5

فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ

So as for Thamud, they were destroyed by the overpowering [blast].

Ayah 6

وَأَمَّا عَادٌۭ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِرِيحٍۢ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍۢ

And as for 'Aad, they were destroyed by a screaming, violent wind

Ayah 7

سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَبْعَ لَيَالٍۢ وَثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومًۭا فَتَرَى ٱلْقَوْمَ فِيهَا صَرْعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ خَاوِيَةٍۢ

Which Allah imposed upon them for seven nights and eight days in succession, so you would see the people therein fallen as if they were hollow trunks of palm trees.

Ayah 8

فَهَلْ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٍۢ

Then do you see of them any remains?

Ayah 9

وَجَآءَ فِرْعَوْنُ وَمَن قَبْلَهُۥ وَٱلْمُؤْتَفِكَٰتُ بِٱلْخَاطِئَةِ

And there came Pharaoh and those before him and the overturned cities with sin.

Ayah 10

فَعَصَوْا۟ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمْ فَأَخَذَهُمْ أَخْذَةًۭ رَّابِيَةً

And they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, so He seized them with a seizure exceeding [in severity].

Ayah 11

إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلْمَآءُ حَمَلْنَٰكُمْ فِى ٱلْجَارِيَةِ

Indeed, when the water overflowed, We carried your ancestors in the sailing ship

Ayah 12

لِنَجْعَلَهَا لَكُمْ تَذْكِرَةًۭ وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنٌۭ وَٰعِيَةٌۭ

That We might make it for you a reminder and [that] a conscious ear would be conscious of it.

Ayah 13

فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ نَفْخَةٌۭ وَٰحِدَةٌۭ

Then when the Horn is blown with one blast

Ayah 14

وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَٱلْجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ

And the earth and the mountains are lifted and leveled with one blow -

Ayah 15

فَيَوْمَئِذٍۢ وَقَعَتِ ٱلْوَاقِعَةُ

Then on that Day, the Resurrection will occur,

Ayah 16

وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِىَ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ وَاهِيَةٌۭ

And the heaven will split [open], for that Day it is infirm.

Ayah 17

وَٱلْمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرْجَآئِهَا ۚ وَيَحْمِلُ عَرْشَ رَبِّكَ فَوْقَهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ ثَمَٰنِيَةٌۭ

And the angels are at its edges. And there will bear the Throne of your Lord above them, that Day, eight [of them].

Ayah 18

يَوْمَئِذٍۢ تُعْرَضُونَ لَا تَخْفَىٰ مِنكُمْ خَافِيَةٌۭ

That Day, you will be exhibited [for judgement]; not hidden among you is anything concealed.

Ayah 19

فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقْرَءُوا۟ كِتَٰبِيَهْ

So as for he who is given his record in his right hand, he will say, "Here, read my record!

Ayah 20

إِنِّى ظَنَنتُ أَنِّى مُلَٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهْ

Indeed, I was certain that I would be meeting my account."

Ayah 21

فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍۢ رَّاضِيَةٍۢ

So he will be in a pleasant life -

Ayah 22

فِى جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍۢ

In an elevated garden,

Ayah 23

قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةٌۭ

Its [fruit] to be picked hanging near.

Ayah 24

كُلُوا۟ وَٱشْرَبُوا۟ هَنِيٓـًٔۢا بِمَآ أَسْلَفْتُمْ فِى ٱلْأَيَّامِ ٱلْخَالِيَةِ

[They will be told], "Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you put forth in the days past."

Ayah 25

وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيْتَنِى لَمْ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهْ

But as for he who is given his record in his left hand, he will say, "Oh, I wish I had not been given my record

Ayah 26

وَلَمْ أَدْرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهْ

And had not known what is my account.

Ayah 27

يَٰلَيْتَهَا كَانَتِ ٱلْقَاضِيَةَ

I wish my death had been the decisive one.

Ayah 28

مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنِّى مَالِيَهْ ۜ

My wealth has not availed me.

Ayah 29

هَلَكَ عَنِّى سُلْطَٰنِيَهْ

Gone from me is my authority."

Ayah 30

خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ

[Allah will say], "Seize him and shackle him.

Ayah 31

ثُمَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ

Then into Hellfire drive him.

Ayah 32

ثُمَّ فِى سِلْسِلَةٍۢ ذَرْعُهَا سَبْعُونَ ذِرَاعًۭا فَٱسْلُكُوهُ

Then into a chain whose length is seventy cubits insert him."

Ayah 33

إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لَا يُؤْمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ

Indeed, he did not used to believe in Allah, the Most Great,

Ayah 34

وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ

Nor did he encourage the feeding of the poor.

Ayah 35

فَلَيْسَ لَهُ ٱلْيَوْمَ هَٰهُنَا حَمِيمٌۭ

So there is not for him here this Day any devoted friend

Ayah 36

وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنْ غِسْلِينٍۢ

Nor any food except from the discharge of wounds;

Ayah 37

لَّا يَأْكُلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلْخَٰطِـُٔونَ

None will eat it except the sinners.

Ayah 38

فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِمَا تُبْصِرُونَ

So I swear by what you see

Ayah 39

وَمَا لَا تُبْصِرُونَ

And what you do not see

Ayah 40

إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍۢ كَرِيمٍۢ

[That] indeed, the Qur'an is the word of a noble Messenger.

Ayah 41

وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَاعِرٍۢ ۚ قَلِيلًۭا مَّا تُؤْمِنُونَ

And it is not the word of a poet; little do you believe.

Ayah 42

وَلَا بِقَوْلِ كَاهِنٍۢ ۚ قَلِيلًۭا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Nor the word of a soothsayer; little do you remember.

Ayah 43

تَنزِيلٌۭ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

[It is] a revelation from the Lord of the worlds.

Ayah 44

وَلَوْ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيْنَا بَعْضَ ٱلْأَقَاوِيلِ

And if Muhammad had made up about Us some [false] sayings,

Ayah 45

لَأَخَذْنَا مِنْهُ بِٱلْيَمِينِ

We would have seized him by the right hand;

Ayah 46

ثُمَّ لَقَطَعْنَا مِنْهُ ٱلْوَتِينَ

Then We would have cut from him the aorta.

Ayah 47

فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنْ أَحَدٍ عَنْهُ حَٰجِزِينَ

And there is no one of you who could prevent [Us] from him.

Ayah 48

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذْكِرَةٌۭ لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ

And indeed, the Qur'an is a reminder for the righteous.

Ayah 49

وَإِنَّا لَنَعْلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ

And indeed, We know that among you are deniers.

Ayah 50

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسْرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ

And indeed, it will be [a cause of] regret upon the disbelievers.

Ayah 51

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَقُّ ٱلْيَقِينِ

And indeed, it is the truth of certainty.

Ayah 52

فَسَبِّحْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلْعَظِيمِ

So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

Surah Al-Haaqqa — Frequently Asked Questions

How many ayahs are in Surah Al-Haaqqa?

Surah Al-Haaqqa has 52 ayahs. It is surah number 69 of the Holy Quran.

Where was Surah Al-Haaqqa revealed?

Surah Al-Haaqqa is a Makki surah — it was revealed in Makkah before the Hijrah to Madinah..

What does the name Al-Haaqqa mean?

The name Al-Haaqqa means “The Reality” in English.
