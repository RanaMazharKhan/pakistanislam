🤲 Duas of Travel and Provision

The dua for a journey, asking for provision, repaying a debt, and freedom from worry and grief.

Duas

The dua for travel (when mounting a vehicle)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ

"In the name of Allah, and all praise is for Allah. Glory to the One who has subjected this to us, and we could never have done it ourselves; and to our Lord we shall surely return."

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud, Book of Jihad

The dua for entering a city or town
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ السَّمَاوَاتِ السَّبْعِ وَمَا أَظْلَلْنَ وَرَبَّ الْأَرَضِينَ السَّبْعِ وَمَا أَقْلَلْنَ

"O Allah, Lord of the seven heavens and all that they shade, and Lord of the seven earths and all that they carry..." (It is reported in the Mustadrak of Al-Hakim; asking Allah for this place's goodness and seeking His protection from its evil.)

The dua for protection from poverty and need
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكُفْرِ وَالْفَقْرِ

"O Allah! I seek Your protection from disbelief and from poverty."

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud, Book of Prayer

The dua for repaying a debt
اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ

"O Allah! Make Your lawful provision sufficient for me instead of the unlawful, and enrich me by Your bounty so that I am in need of no one but You."

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Book of Supplications

The dua for freedom from worry and grief
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

"O Allah! I seek Your protection from worry and grief, from helplessness and laziness, from miserliness and cowardice, from the burden of debt, and from being overpowered by people."

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Supplications

The dua for lawful provision
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

"O Allah! I ask You for beneficial knowledge, pure provision, and deeds that are accepted."

Reference: Sunan Ibn Majah, Book of Establishing the Prayer
