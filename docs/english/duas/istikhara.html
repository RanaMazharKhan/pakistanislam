🤲 Dua-e-Istikhara — Asking Allah for What Is Good

Istikhara means asking Allah for what is good. When an important decision has to be made — marriage, a job, business, travel or study — the Prophet (peace be upon him) taught us to seek Allah's guidance through this dua. This page gives the full Arabic text, a clear English translation and the correct way to perform it.

The Dua

Dua-e-Istikhara — Arabic text
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ وَأَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ الْعَظِيمِ، فَإِنَّكَ تَقْدِرُ وَلَا أَقْدِرُ وَتَعْلَمُ وَلَا أَعْلَمُ وَأَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ خَيْرٌ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي وَعَاجِلِهِ وَآجِلِهِ، فَاقْدُرْهُ لِي وَيَسِّرْهُ لِي ثُمَّ بَارِكْ لِي فِيهِ، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ شَرٌّ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي وَعَاجِلِهِ وَآجِلِهِ، فَاصْرِفْهُ عَنِّي وَاصْرِفْنِي عَنْهُ وَاقْدُرْ لِي الْخَيْرَ حَيْثُ كَانَ ثُمَّ أَرْضِنِي بِهِ

O Allah, I seek what is good from You through Your knowledge, and I seek strength through Your power, and I ask You for Your great bounty. Indeed, You have power and I do not, You know and I do not know, and You are the Knower of the unseen. O Allah, if You know that this matter is good for me in my religion, my livelihood and the outcome of my affairs — now and later — then decree it for me, make it easy for me and bless it for me. And if You know that this matter is bad for me in my religion, my livelihood and the outcome of my affairs — now and later — then turn it away from me and turn me away from it, and decree for me what is good wherever it may be, and make me content with it.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari — the dua of Istikhara is narrated from Jabir ibn Abdullah (may Allah be pleased with him).

How to perform Istikhara

1. Wudu and intention: perform wudu and intend that you are praying two rak'ahs for Istikhara.

2. Two rak'ahs of voluntary prayer: pray two rak'ahs apart from the obligatory prayers.

3. The dua: after the prayer, recite the Dua of Istikhara and keep in mind the matter you need to decide, in place of the words "this matter".

4. Trust in Allah: then decide with trust in Allah — the side your heart settles towards, or the matter that becomes easy, is the better one.

Must a dream come after Istikhara?

No. Istikhara is not about waiting for a dream, a colour or a sign. Its purpose is to ask Allah for what is good; after that, the heart settling towards one side, or the matter becoming easy or blocked, is itself the guidance. If your heart is not settled, you may repeat Istikhara.

When to perform Istikhara

Before any important decision, at any time of day or night. Avoid praying voluntary prayers at the forbidden times — sunrise, sunset and the sun's zenith — though the dua itself may be made at any time.

Common Questions

How is Istikhara performed?

Perform wudu, pray two rak'ahs of voluntary prayer, then recite the Dua of Istikhara and keep in mind the matter you need to decide. The Prophet (peace be upon him) taught: when one of you intends to do something, let him pray two rak'ahs apart from the obligatory prayers and then say this dua (Sahih al-Bukhari).

Must a dream come after Istikhara?

No. Istikhara is not about waiting for a dream, a colour or a sign. After Istikhara, guidance comes through the heart settling towards one side, or the matter becoming easy or blocked.

Can someone else perform Istikhara for me?

It is better to perform it yourself, because Istikhara is asking Allah directly for guidance in your own matter. Having someone else pray for you is not a substitute for it.

How many times can Istikhara be repeated?

If your heart is not settled the first time, you may repeat it. There is no fixed number; keep asking Allah for what is good until you feel at ease about the decision.
