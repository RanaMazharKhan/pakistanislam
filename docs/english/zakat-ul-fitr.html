🌙 Zakat-ul-Fitr (Fitrana) 2027

Zakat-ul-Fitr — known in Pakistan as Fitrana or Sadqa-e-Fitr — is the obligatory charity given at the end of Ramadan. Its purpose is that no poor person goes hungry on Eid day, and every home shares in the joy of Eid. It is due from every Muslim who owns the nisab, and it must be paid before the Eid prayer.

When to pay Fitrana in 2027?

Eid-ul-Fitr 2027 is expected on 9–10 March 2027 (1 Shawwal 1448 AH). Fitrana can be paid in the last days of Ramadan or any time before Eid — it is best to pay it in the last ten days of Ramadan so that it reaches the needy before Eid. It must be paid before the Eid prayer; money given after the prayer counts as ordinary charity, not Fitrana.

How much is Fitrana?

The amount is fixed per person by measure: half a sa' of wheat (about 2 kg) or one sa' of barley, dates or raisins (about 4 kg) — or their cash value. The Council of Islamic Ideology in Pakistan set the minimum amount for 2026 at Rs 300 per person (on the price of wheat). The 2027 amount will be announced during Ramadan 2027 and will be updated on this page — check this page again in the last ten days of Ramadan, or ask your local masjid.

Who must pay Fitrana?

Fitrana is due from every Muslim who owns wealth beyond basic needs up to the nisab — man or woman. The head of the household also pays on behalf of minor children, so that Fitrana is paid for every member of the home.

Who should receive Fitrana?

Fitrana goes to the same people who are eligible for Zakat — the poor and the needy who do not have enough for their needs. It cannot be given to parents, grandparents, children or a spouse; giving it to a poor relative carries a double reward — charity and keeping family ties.

Common Questions

How much is Fitrana in 2027?

The amount is fixed by measure: half a sa' of wheat (about 2 kg) or one sa' of barley, dates or raisins (about 4 kg) per person, or their cash value. In 2026 the minimum amount in Pakistan was Rs 300 per person; the 2027 amount will be announced during Ramadan 2027 and updated here.

By when should Fitrana be paid?

Before the Eid-ul-Fitr prayer. It is best to pay it in the last ten days of Ramadan so that it reaches the needy before Eid.

Do I have to pay Fitrana for my children?

Yes — the head of the household pays Fitrana on behalf of minor children as well.

Who cannot be given Fitrana?

Parents, grandparents, children and a spouse cannot be given Fitrana. It is the right of the poor and the needy.
