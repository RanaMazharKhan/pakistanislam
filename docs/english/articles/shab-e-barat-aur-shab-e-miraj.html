Shab-e-Barat and Shab-e-Miraj

These are two blessed nights of the Islamic year — Shab-e-Miraj (the night of 27 Rajab) and Shab-e-Barat (the night of 15 Sha'ban). In 2027, Shab-e-Miraj is expected on the night of 5–6 January and Shab-e-Barat on the night of 23–24 January (the final dates will be announced by the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee).

Shab-e-Miraj — The Night of the Ascension

On this night the Prophet (peace be upon him) was taken from Makkah to Jerusalem, and from there to the heavens. The gift of the five prayers was given on this very occasion. On this night, nafl prayer, recitation of the Quran and sending durood are good acts.

Shab-e-Barat — The Night of Deliverance

The 15th night of Sha'ban is called the "Night of Deliverance" (Barat). According to ahadith, on this night Allah Almighty forgives a great many of His servants — except those who associate partners with Allah and those who harbour malice. Therefore, on this night, pray for forgiveness for yourself and make peace with others.

What Should Be Done on These Nights?

There is no fixed, specified worship for these nights — but nafl prayers, recitation of the Quran, dhikr, dua and seeking forgiveness are all proven good deeds. According to some scholars, fasting the next day is also recommended. It is better to avoid innovations and unnecessary formalities, and to worship with sincerity.
