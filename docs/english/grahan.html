🌙 Solar & Lunar Eclipses — When and at What Time in Pakistan?

An eclipse of the sun or the moon is one of the signs of Allah Almighty. According to the blessed Hadith, the sun and the moon do not eclipse because of anyone's death or life (birth) — when you see an eclipse, turn to the remembrance of Allah, dua and prayer (Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Eclipses; Sahih Muslim). This page gives the verified timings (PKT) of the coming eclipses in Pakistan, the method of Salat al-Kusuf and Salat al-Khusuf, and the prescribed acts. Timings source: timeanddate.com.

🌙 Lunar Eclipse — Sunday 21 February 2027

This will be a penumbral lunar eclipse: the moon will not enter the Earth's dark shadow, so the moon will not disappear; instead a slight dimming/shadow will be felt on one part of it. The timings will be approximately the same across Pakistan:

The final announcement of Ramadan's dates is made by the Central Ruet-e-Hilal Committee, so the Hijri date may differ by one day. On this night, special attention to Salat al-Khusuf and dua is fitting — the method is given below.

☀️ Solar Eclipse — Monday 2 August 2027

This will be one of the world's most famous total solar eclipses (totality will be visible in Egypt, Libya, Saudi Arabia and elsewhere) — but in Pakistan it will be only partial; nowhere in Pakistan will there be complete darkness. Across the country, the eclipse will be visible at each city's own times, roughly between 2:43 pm and 4:51 pm in the afternoon:

⚠️ Safety advice: Never look at a solar eclipse with the naked eye, ordinary sunglasses, X-ray film or a mobile phone camera — the eyes can be permanently damaged. Use only certified eclipse glasses, or observe indirectly, for example with a pinhole projector. (A lunar eclipse is safe to watch with the naked eye.)

🌕 The Notable Lunar Eclipses of 2028

Partial lunar eclipse — 6–7 July 2028

At night, about 39% of the moon will pass into the Earth's dark shadow. Karachi timings: the partial phase begins at 10:09 pm, the greatest eclipse at 11:19 pm, and the partial phase ends at 12:30 am (the penumbral phase runs from 8:44 pm to 1:55 am). It will be easy to watch across Pakistan.

Total lunar eclipse — 31 December 2028

This will be the most notable event of the period: in the evening the moon will pass into total eclipse and appear red (a "Blood Moon"). Karachi timings: the partial phase begins at 8:07 pm, totality from 9:16 to 10:27 pm (about 71 minutes), the greatest eclipse at 9:52 pm, and the whole sequence continues until 12:40 am. It is expected to fall a night or two before Shab-e-Barat 1450 AH (the final date will be settled by the sighting of the moon).

🕌 The Method of Salat al-Kusuf and Salat al-Khusuf

Salat al-Kusuf is the prayer of the solar eclipse and Salat al-Khusuf that of the lunar eclipse. The Prophet (peace be upon him) himself prayed this prayer at the time of an eclipse and encouraged the Ummah towards prayer, dua, seeking forgiveness, takbir and charity (Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Eclipses; Sahih Muslim). This prayer is Sunnah, not obligatory.

The solar eclipse prayer (Kusuf) — the Hanafi method

• Two rak'ahs are prayed in congregation in the masjid, behind the imam.
• The prayer is like an ordinary two rak'ahs — one ruku' and two sajdahs in each rak'ah.
• There is no adhan or iqamah for this prayer; the announcement is made by calling "اَلصَّلٰوةُ جَامِعَةٌ" (as-salatu jami'ah — come to the congregational prayer).
• According to Imam Abu Hanifah (may Allah have mercy on him), the recitation in this prayer is done in a quiet voice.
• The time of the prayer lasts from the start of the eclipse until it clears completely; try to remain busy in prayer, dua and remembrance during the eclipse.

The lunar eclipse prayer (Khusuf) — the Hanafi method

• According to the Hanafi school, the lunar eclipse prayer is prayed individually (at home) as two rak'ahs — it is not arranged in congregation (Al-Kasani, Bada'i as-Sana'i).
• After the prayer, spend the time in dua and seeking forgiveness for as long as the eclipse lasts.

This summary is for general guidance; it is better to confirm with the imam of your local masjid or a trusted scholar, because the schools of law differ on some details.

🤲 Prescribed Acts at the Time of an Eclipse

In the light of the Hadith, the prescribed acts during an eclipse are: praying Salat al-Kusuf/Khusuf, making abundant dua and istighfar (seeking forgiveness), takbir and remembrance, and giving charity — until the eclipse ends. For more duas, see our Masnoon Duas page.

❓ Common Questions

When is the next lunar eclipse in Pakistan?

Sunday 21 February 2027 (penumbral) — from 2:12 am to 6:13 am. After that, a notable partial lunar eclipse on 6–7 July 2028 and a total lunar eclipse on 31 December 2028.

When is the next solar eclipse in Pakistan?

Monday 2 August 2027 — a partial eclipse. In Karachi from 3:01 pm to 4:48 pm (greatest at 3:57 pm, about 30% covered), in Lahore from 3:16 pm to 4:25 pm.

Is an eclipse harmful for pregnant women or children?

There is no basis for this belief in Islam. According to the Hadith, an eclipse does not happen because of anyone's birth or death — it is among the signs of Allah. The only real precaution is not to look at a solar eclipse with the naked eye.

How long does the time of the eclipse prayer last?

As long as the eclipse lasts. As soon as the eclipse clears, the time of the prayer ends; if you could not pray during it, make dua and seek forgiveness.

Can a lunar eclipse be watched with the naked eye?

Yes — watching a lunar eclipse with the naked eye is completely safe. The restriction is only on looking at a solar eclipse.
