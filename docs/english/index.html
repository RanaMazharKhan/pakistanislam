How Are Prayer Times Calculated?

Timings use the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (18° for Fajr and Isha) with Hanafi Asr — the method used in most Pakistani cities. Jafria timings are shown separately on each city page. The Islamic day starts at Maghrib, not midnight, so the Hijri date changes after Maghrib.

Hanafi vs Jafria

What is the difference?

Asr is later in the Hanafi method, and Jafria Iftar is a few minutes after Maghrib, once the redness in the sky has gone. Fajr and Dhuhr are the same in both.
