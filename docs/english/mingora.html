About Mingora Timings

Prayer times in Mingora are based on the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (18 degrees for Fajr and Isha) with Hanafi Asr — the method commonly used across Pakistan. Sehri ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib. The Islamic date changes after Maghrib, so after Maghrib this page shows the next Hijri date. Timings are calculated and updated daily — please confirm with your local masjid.

Mingora — Nawafil Times (Ishraq, Chasht, Zawal, Tahajjud)

Ishraq time — Mingora

Ishraq starts about 20 minutes after sunrise — today in Mingora at about 06:57 (sunrise 06:37). This is also the start of Chasht (Duha) prayer.

Chasht (Duha) time — Mingora

Chasht runs from after Ishraq until Zawal; the best time is when the sun is well up — today between 06:37 and 11:54.

Zawal time — Mingora

Zawal is the moment the sun passes directly overhead — a few minutes before Dhuhr (11:54). Prayer is disliked at that moment; wait for Dhuhr to begin.

Tahajjud time — Mingora

Tahajjud is from after Isha (18:38) until before Fajr (05:10) — the best time is the last part of the night.

These Nawafil times follow the general rules and are approximate — ask your masjid for full guidance.
