🕐 How Sehri and Iftar Work

Sehri ends before the Fajr adhan — that is, the last time for Sehri is the time of Fajr. Iftar is at Maghrib, as soon as the sun sets. In the Jafria school, Iftar is after the redness disappears following Maghrib, so it is a few minutes later — both timings are available on every city's page.

🌟 Preparing for Ramadan

Before Ramadan, save your city's timetable, check it against your masjid's timings, and make sure to listen for the Ruet-e-Hilal announcement on Chand Raat — because the final date of the first fast is decided only after that announcement. For Sehri, dates, water and light food are better, and breaking the fast with dates and water is the Sunnah.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will Ramadan 2027 begin?

8 February 2027 is expected; 9 February is also possible in Pakistan. The final announcement will come from the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee.

Until when can Sehri be eaten?

Until the time of Fajr. Once the time of Fajr has passed, the time of Sehri is over — so it is better to stop 5 to 10 minutes earlier.

Why is Jafria Iftar later?

In the Jafria school, Iftar is after the redness disappears following Maghrib, so there is a difference of a few minutes.
