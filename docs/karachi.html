Timings Ke Baare Me

Karachi ki timing doosre shehron se kyun farq hai?

Har sheher ki jagah alag hai, is liye sooraj nikalne aur doobne ka waqt alag hota hai. Is liye har sheher ki apni timing hoti hai.

Hanafi aur Jafria me farq kya hai?

Asr (Hanafi me saaya 2 guna) aur Iftar (Jafria me Maghrib ke baad surkhi khatam hone par) me chand minute ka farq hota hai.

Ye timings kitni durust hain?

Timings Karachi method se calculate hoti hain. Apni local masjid ki timing ko tarjeeh den.
