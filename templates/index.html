📖 Namaz Timings Kaise Banti Hain?

Sooraj ki harkat se — har namaz ka apna usool

Fajr us waqt jab sooraj ufuq se 18 degree neeche ho aur subah ki safedi phailne lage. Zohar sooraj dhalne ke baad. Asr saaye se napi jati hai — Hanafi tareeqe me saaya 2 guna hone par. Maghrib sooraj ghuroob hote hi. Isha jab Maghrib ki surkhi poori khatam ho jaye (18 degree). Har sheher ki jagah alag hai, is liye timings me 30–45 minute tak ka farq ho sakta hai — isi liye har sheher ka apna page zaroori hai.

Hanafi vs Jafria — farq kya hai?

Dono ka Fajr aur Zohar taqreeban aik jesa hota hai. Farq do jagah hai: Asr (Hanafi me saaya 2 guna, is liye late) aur Iftar (Jafria me Maghrib ke baad surkhi khatam hone par, is liye chand minute late). Is site par har sheher ke page par dono timings maujood hain taake har firqe ke log apni timing dekh saken.

🌙 Maghrib Rule — Islamic date kab badalti hai?

Islami din Maghrib se shuru hota hai, aadhi raat se nahi. Is liye is site par Maghrib ke baad hi nayi Hijri date dikhai jati hai — jabke aksar websites date aadhi raat ko badalti hain aur shaam ko ghalat date dikhati hain. Yehi is site ka sab se bara farq hai.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

Islamic date kaise maloom hoti hai?

Islamic mahina chand nazar aane se shuru hota hai. Pakistan me faisla Markazi Ruet-e-Hilal Committee karti hai — isi liye official date kabhi calculated date se 1 din farq ho sakti hai.

Namaz timings ka method kya hai?

University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi (18°/18°) aur Hanafi Asr. Jafria timings alag table me hain.

Ramadan 2027 kab shuru hoga?

8 February 2027 mutawaqqa hai, Pakistan me 9 February bhi mumkin hai. Final elaan Ruet-e-Hilal se hoga.

Jafria iftar Hanafi se late kyun hota hai?

Jafria fiqh me iftar us waqt hota hai jab Maghrib ke baad surkhi khatam ho jaye, is liye chand minute ka farq hota hai.

Apne sheher ki timing kaise dhoonden?

Is page par search box me sheher ka naam likhen ya table me us ke link par click karen.

Ye timings kitni durust hain?

Timings Karachi method se calculate hoti hain aur rozana update hoti hain, lekin maqami farq ho sakta hai — apni local masjid ki timing ko tarjeeh den.
